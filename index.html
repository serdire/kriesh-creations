Behind the Scissors

"A handmade gift carries the pulse of the person who made it."

Welcome to Kriesh Creations. What started as an intimate passion for tactile paper art and delicate keepsakes has blossomed into an atelier that has touched over 1,670 celebrations across the globe.

In a world of factory-printed generic gifts, I believe the moments that truly stay with us are the ones crafted by human hands. Every fold in our celebration boxes, every arranged outfit in our groom trousseau suites, and every royal silk ribbon is placed with intentional tenderness.

Whether you are celebrating a special milestone, preparing wedding trousseau hampers for your big day, or gifting someone a piece of your heart—I look forward to crafting your story.

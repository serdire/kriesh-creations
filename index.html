Behind the Scissors

"A handmade gift carries the pulse of the person who made it."

Welcome to Kriesh Creations. What started as an intimate passion for tactile paper art and delicate keepsakes has blossomed into an atelier that has touched over 1,600 celebrations across the globe.

In a world of factory-printed generic gifts, I believe the moments that truly stay with us are the ones crafted by human hands. Every fold in our explosion boxes, every gold leaf placed in our resin pieces, and every silk bow on our trousseau trays is placed with intentional tenderness.

Whether you are celebrating a 50th wedding anniversary, preparing wedding trousseau hampers for your big day, or gifting someone a piece of your heart—I look forward to crafting your story.

# Kriesh Creations — Bespoke Handcrafted Atelier

> Customised handmade gifts, explosion cards, floral resin wall art, royal trousseau hampers, and home decor by **Monica Maheshwari**.

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Design Archetype
* **Archetype**: **Artisan / Heritage / Organic Luxury**
* **Palette**: Warm Linen (`#FAF7F2`), Baked Terracotta (`#C25E40`), Dried Sage (`#5B6E58`), Antique Brass (`#C5A059`), and Warm Espresso Stone (`#1C1917`).
* **Typography**: `Cormorant Garamond` (High-contrast editorial serif) + `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Clean humanist body).
* **Craft Textures**: Organic paper grain, wax seal insignia, polaroid product framing, and soft pill badges.

---

## ✨ Features
1. **Interactive Curated Collections**: Seamless client-side filtering across Explosion Boxes, Resin Wall Art, Wedding Trousseau Packaging, and Custom Hampers.
2. **Interactive Bespoke Studio**: A 4-step custom order configurator that dynamically constructs a structured inquiry and sends it straight to Monica's atelier on WhatsApp.
3. **The Craft Journey**: A 4-phase visual timeline explaining the bespoke process from consultation to secure worldwide delivery.
4. **Authentic Storytelling**: Real creator notes, verified patron testimonials, and genuine brand context from [@kriesh_creations](https://www.instagram.com/kriesh_creations/).
